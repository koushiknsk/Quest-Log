01 DAILY SCORING (MAX 100 PTS)

Points are awarded daily based on the percentage of scheduled protocols completed:

Daily Score = (Completed Directives / Scheduled Directives) × 100 PTS

⚠️ Daily Finalizer: Scores are calculated and permanently locked at 11:59 PM every night. No retroactive score changes are permitted after finalization.

02 1UP STREAK SHIELDS (MAX 3)

Complete 2 consecutive perfect weeks (14 straight days with a 100 Daily Score) to earn 1 Shield 🛡️.

  • Max Inventory: You can hold up to 3 visual shields (🛡️ 🛡️ 🛡️).
  • No Idle Glow: Active earned shields in inventory have no glow in normal conditions.
  • Actionable Static Warning Glow: On Day N+1, if Day N had a partial completion or no-show, the available shield displays a static neon glow (no dynamic breathing/pulse in any case).
  • Day N+1 Muted Inventory State: When a shield is deployed on Day N+1, it remains visible in the inventory slot but muted with color.
  • Day N+2 Removal: On Day N+2, the consumed shield is permanently removed from the inventory.
  • 1-Day Miss Rule: A shield can only be applied if you missed exactly 1 day.
  • Weekly Limit: Maximum 1 shield can be used per calendar week.
  • Shield Generation Exclusion: Any week where a shield is used is not counted toward generating new shields.
  • 2-Day Missed Break: If 2 consecutive days are missed, shields cannot be used to restore the streak; your streak breaks and consistency multiplier resets to 0.
03 WEEKLY CONSISTENCY BONUS (x)

Maintain unbreaking 100% daily consistency across calendar weeks to earn compounding bonus points:

WEEK 1 PERFECT +10 PTS (x = 10)
WEEK 2 PERFECT +15 PTS (x = 15)
WEEK 3 PERFECT +20 PTS (x = 20)
WEEK N PERFECT +(Previous + 5) PTS

🛡️ Shield Usage: When a shield is used on a missed day, consistency is preserved (x value does not become 0), but no bonus is added for that shielded week. If 2 consecutive days are missed or consistency breaks without a shield, x resets to 0.
